Lacrosse Origins

To Indigenous nations, lacrosse was a sacred gift known as "The Medicine Game," played to heal the sick, honor the Creator, and peacefully resolve territorial disputes as the "Little Brother of War." Unlike today's organized sport, traditional matches took place across vast, boundaryless landscapes stretching for miles, with trees or rocks serving as goals. Handcrafted wooden sticks and stuffed deerskin balls were used by hundreds of competing men, bound by the core rule to never touch the ball by hand. Accompanied by sacred dances and community feasts, these multi-day contests stood as enduring tests of resilience and cultural unity.
