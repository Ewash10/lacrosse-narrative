Lacrosse: A Hypertext Narrative

Originally played by Indigenous nations as "The Medicine Game" for spiritual healing, community unity, and honoring the Creator, lacrosse has evolved from sacred tradition into one of the fastest-growing modern sports. In the 21st century, the game has surged beyond its historic regional roots through rapid expansion preparing for return to the Olympic stage in 2028. Long before it filled college stadiums, lacrosse was known to Indigenous nations as "The Medicine Game." This site traces that journey — click a chapter below to begin.
